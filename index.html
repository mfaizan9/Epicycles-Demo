Orbits and Traced Paths

Description & Controls

In this animation the blue and red objects revolve around the yellow object in circlular orbits, shown in black. The red tracks are the paths that result when the system moves to make the selected object appear stationary.

This demonstrates that for simple circular orbits the heliocentric and geocentric models are equivalent, at least for predictive purposes.
